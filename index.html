The Gap

Masvingo has the talent.
It's missing the connection.

Across Masvingo Province, developers are building in isolation. Students are learning without mentors. Entrepreneurs are innovating without networks. The talent is here — what's missing is a central place to connect, collaborate, and grow together.

Working Alone

Skilled developers and designers with no local network to learn from or build alongside.

Missed Opportunities

Students and graduates without mentorship or a clear path from classroom to career.

No Central Hub

No shared space where ideas, skills, and collaboration naturally happen — until now.

See how we're closing the gap

Focus Areas

Where we're building

Six areas where Masvingo's tech community is starting to focus its energy.

Software Development

Building applications, systems, and tools that solve real problems — from web and mobile to enterprise software.

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring machine learning, automation, and AI applications built for African contexts and challenges.

Drones & GIS

Mapping, surveying, and aerial technology for agriculture, land use, and infrastructure planning.

Entrepreneurship & Startups

Supporting founders turning ideas into ventures — from first prototype to first customer.

Digital Skills

Practical training in coding, design, and digital literacy for students and career-changers.

Agriculture, Tourism & Industry

Applying technology to Masvingo's core industries — from heritage tourism to smart farming.
